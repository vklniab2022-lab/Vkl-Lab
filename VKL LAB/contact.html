Join Our Team

We are always looking for passionate researchers to join our lab in organoid engineering and biosensors.

Ph.D. Opportunities

Students interested in pursuing a Ph.D. in Organoid Engineering, Stem Cells, or Biosensing can apply through the i3C-BRIC-RCB Joint Ph.D. Programme. Selected candidates will work on active sponsored projects.

  • Eligibility: M.Sc./M.Tech in Biotechnology, Nanotechnology, or related fields.
  • Must have cleared national exams (CSIR-UGC NET, DBT-BET, DST-INSPIRE, or RCB entrance).

Dissertation / Internship

We welcome dissertation students, interns, and project associates interested in hands-on research experience in organoid biology, biomaterials, 3D bioprinting, and biosensing. Short-term and long-term placements are available.

  • M.Sc. / M.Tech dissertation students from recognized universities.
  • Summer internship applications open each January for May–July intake.
